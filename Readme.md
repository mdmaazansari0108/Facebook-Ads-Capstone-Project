# Verdant Pure - Full-Funnel Meta Ads & Growth Capstone

Author Mohammad Maaz Ansari  Brand Verdant Pure (D2C Skincare)  Duration 15 Days[cite 43]

## Project Overview
An end-to-end performance marketing capstone building a full-funnel Meta Ads strategy, GA4 attribution setup, and Looker Studio BI dashboard for Verdant Pure, a botanical skincare brand tailored for urban Indian skin[cite 43, 44].

 Total Spend ₹7,000 (₹1,000day CBO)[cite 44]
 Total Revenue ₹16,182 (18 Purchases @ ₹899 AOV)[cite 44]
 Blended ROAS 2.31x (Peaking at 3.85x in BOF retargeting)[cite 44, 48]
 Blended CPA ₹389 (19% below the ₹480 break-even target)[cite 44]

---

## Execution Phases

### Phase 1 Foundation & Market Research (Days 1–5)[cite 43]
 Audit & Hero SKUs Validated high search intent (246K+ monthly searches) across competitors like Mamaearth and Pilgrim, selecting three core products 10% Vitamin C Serum, Salicylic Acid Facewash, and Herbal Tan Removal Soap[cite 44, 45].
 Targeting & Creative Segmented urban Indian shoppers (ages 18–35) plus Lookalikeretargeting pools, mapping 45 Single Images, 3-Card Routine Carousels, and 15s UGC Reels[cite 44, 46].

### Phase 2 Technical Setup & Launch Prep (Days 6–10)[cite 43]
 Tracking & Attribution Configured `Verdent_Pure_Pixel` (`28984721104488152`) with Automatic Advanced Matching, 4 standard funnel events, and dynamic GA4 UTM tags[cite 47].
 Campaign Build Structured a ₹1,000day CBO campaign split 70% TOF, 20% MOF, and 10% BOF (featuring the `PURE20` 20% off offer)[cite 44, 46, 47].

### Phase 3 Analytics, BI & Scaling (Days 11–14)[cite 43]
 Creative AB Winner 45 Single Image (`2.49x` blended ROAS; `6.74x` BOF peak) outperformed the 3-Card Carousel (`1.69x` ROAS; `0` BOF orders)[cite 44, 49].
 Looker Studio & Governance Built an executive BI dashboard using custom `SUM()` calculated fields and established a rule-based optimization tree (`CTR  1.0%`, `Frequency  2.8`, `CPA  ₹480`, `ROAS  2.5x` scaling by `+20%` every 48h)[cite 50, 51].

---

## Next-Phase Scaling Roadmap
1. Shift 100% of BOF retargeting spend into high-converting 45 Single Image ads (`6.74x` ROAS)[cite 49, 52].
2. Allocate 60% of future creative production to 15–20s vertical Reels[cite 52].
3. Scale winning MOFBOF budgets by `+20%` every 48 hours without resetting learning[cite 52].
4. Launch Day-21 WhatsApp replenishment flows to boost repeat LTV[cite 52].
